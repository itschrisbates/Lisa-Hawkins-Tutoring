Biography

Lisa Hawkins is a musician, songwriter, and music tutor based in Auckland, New Zealand. She teaches singing and songwriting, working with students of different ages and experience levels in a supportive and encouraging environment.

Her lessons focus on building confidence, musical understanding, and practical skills students can apply to real music. Each lesson is tailored to the individual, helping students develop their voice, creativity, and personal style.
