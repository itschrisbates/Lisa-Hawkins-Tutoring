Lessons

One-on-one music tutoring in Epsom and Central Auckland, for all skill levels. I specialise in singing and songwriting — always tailored to your goals and the music you love.

Singing

Personalised vocal coaching focused on building technique and confidence. We’ll work on the fundamentals (breath, placement, control, and vocal health) and apply it directly to songs you want to sing.

Songwriting

Write better songs and find your voice. We’ll explore melody, lyrics, song structure, and break down great songs to understand what makes them work — then apply it to your own writing.

Format

One-on-one lessons in 30, 45, or 60 minute sessions. Based in Epsom — feel free to ask about availability and scheduling.

Feel free to send an email — I’d love to hear about you and your goals.
